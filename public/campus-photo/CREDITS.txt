Vernadsky campus — architectural reconstruction

The homepage image (vernadsky.webp) is a Blender Cycles render. The
on-demand rotatable model (vernadsky.glb) is exported from the same scene.
The south and east elevations use the photograph credited below. Dimensions,
landscaping, lighting and the surrounding buildings are artistic estimates.
